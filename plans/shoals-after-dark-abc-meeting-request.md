1. **Perimeter Control with ID-Verified Entry:** The event footprint will have a single controlled entry point staffed with trained ID checkers. Every attendee will be age-verified upon entry. Anyone under 21 will be clearly identified.

2. **Wristband Program (21+ Only):** We plan to offer a $30 premium wristband available exclusively to guests aged 21 and over, verified at point of purchase. This wristband will grant expedited entry and streamlined service at all participating bars within the footprint. The purchase and issuance of wristbands will serve as a secondary age-verification layer and will be marketed exclusively in compliance with ABC advertising and marketing regulations — no language will imply that alcohol is included or incentivized beyond lawful parameters.

3. **Secondary In-Venue ID Checks:** In addition to entry verification, participating bar staff will be instructed to check IDs for any guest who appears under 30, consistent with responsible beverage service best practices.

4. **Designated Rideshare Pickup/Drop-off Lane:** We intend to designate a clearly marked rideshare lane on or adjacent to Mobile Street to reduce impaired driving and facilitate safe departure from the event. 5. **Coordination with Law Enforcement:** We are committed to working with Florence PD, city officials, and any other relevant agencies to ensure adequate security presence and crowd management.
